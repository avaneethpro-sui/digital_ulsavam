Led by veteran Chenda Master Sreedharan Marar, this 45-member traditional percussion guild has performed at major temple poorams across Kerala, including Thrissur Pooram and Arattupuzha Pooram.

The ensemble brings full traditional attire (Kachapuram and Melmundu), calibrated brass cymbals (Elathalam), and traditional horns (Kombu). Ideal for high-profile weddings, temple festivals, and cultural inauguration ceremonies.
